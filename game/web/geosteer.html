Keep the lateral in the pay

You steer a 4,620 ft horizontal well through a pay zone about 25 to 40 ft thick. You can't see the beds: you see gamma ray from a sensor 50 ft behind the bit, and you match it against the typelog to guess where you are. Steer up or down one 300 ft interval at a time. The true structure is revealed when you finish.
