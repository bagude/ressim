# Frac pad site equipment: sourced gap fill

Rules: each number has its URL. **[read]** means I read the primary document myself. **[snippet]** means the number came from a search-result excerpt and I have not opened the page, so check it before you use it. Where I found nothing, the entry says "not found".

## 1. Wireline

- **Wireline truck or skid:** no spec sheet for a frac plug-and-perf unit was found. The nearest figures are general-purpose units **[snippet]**:
  - Koller A1-003 fixed-body unit: 4,552 x 2,500 x 2,238 mm, 11,110 kg. https://www.scribd.com/document/541693771/A1-003-Wireline-Unit-FB-15ft
  - Koller A1-004 24 ft logging unit: 10,600 x 2,550 x 3,950 mm, 26,000 kg max. https://www.scribd.com/document/483830534/A1-004-Logging-Unit-FB-24ft
  - Use these as size references only, not as plug-and-perf sizing.
- **Lubricator [read]:** Hunting lightweight lubricator datasheet, https://media.huntingplc.com/products-and-services/well-intervention-equipment/pressure-control-equipment/wireline-pressure-control-equipment/hunting-lightweight-lubricator-datasheet.pdf
  - Sections come in standard lengths of 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 ft.
  - Bore IDs are 3", 4.06" and 5-1/8".
  - Hunting makes integral single-piece lubricators for 15K and lightweight ones for 10K.
  - Weight of a 5-1/8" ID x 10 ft section: conventional 893 lb, integral 831 lb, lightweight 381 lb. A 12 ft integral section weighs 972 lb.
  - The datasheet gives no OD for a 15K section: not found.
- **Grease injection head:** a Phuel 15K grease head has an 8-3/4"-4 quick union and a 5" seal bore **[snippet]**, https://manuals.online/phuel/industrial-equipment/145-4427-hv0/user-manual/12bej. Its length and OD: not found.
- **Wireline BOP:** dimensions not found. Parveen catalogue for reference: https://parveen.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Blowout-Preventers11.pdf
- **Crane:** not found. No source names the crane tonnage used on a frac pad.
- **Plug-and-perf tool string length:** not found.
- **Visible sub-parts:** cab, logging cab with a window facing the well, cable drum, sheaves (top and bottom), lubricator stack (BOP, then lubricator sections, then grease head), grease skid, and hoses.

## 4. Site support

- **Light tower [read]:** Generac MLT6SM spec sheet, https://woodstockpower.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/GM_MLT6SM_SPEC_03-28-18.pdf

- **Sound walls [read]:** Radtke et al. 2017, *Journal of Occupational and Environmental Hygiene* 14(8), https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/210461/cdc_210461_DS1.pdf
  - Colorado frac and drilling sound walls are 16–32 ft (4.9–9.8 m) high.
  - The walls are commonly built from acoustic blankets 8 ft high x 20 ft long on steel frames. Some operators use hay bales instead.
  - The blankets are rated STC 25 and cut equipment noise by 15–22 dBA.
  - The study's sites include a 32 ft wall on a 22-well pad **[snippet]**, https://www.heienergy.org/sites/default/files/mckenzie-comm-noise-exposure.pdf
