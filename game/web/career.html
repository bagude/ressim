Run the asset for five years

You inherit producing wells and open acreage. Build a type curve from the wells you can see, plan pads, win the board's budget each year, and live with what the rock actually delivers.

  • Win: create the most value in 60 months: the cash you generate plus what your wells are still worth.
  • Each year: promise the board an oil volume and a budget, then deliver it. Missing the promise costs confidence, and so does beating it by more than 10%.
  • The yearly review also scores: capex within 105% of budget, proved reserves replaced and not revised down, a clean reserves audit, and emissions under the target.
  • Lose: board confidence falls below 20, or cash runs past the credit line.

            How the schedule works
            • One rig per pad. Wells are batch drilled, each rig gets faster with practice, and rigs move between pads.
            • A frac crew starts a pad only after all its wells are drilled.
            • Wells come online together after a 14-day tie-in.
            • A pad waits for the next budget year if its cost would break that year's budget.
            • Pads lock 30 days before spud.
              A game, not an engineering tool. Economics are illustrative; production comes from a 2D one-layer simulator on the SPE1 benchmark oil.
